# Sleep Channel Launch Plan

## Upload Path Decision

The first real release path is **manual-first upload**, not YouTube API integration.

Why:

- it lets us validate the content loop immediately
- it avoids blocking on Google OAuth and channel API setup
- it is enough for the first private upload, thumbnail review, and title test

## First Channel Identity

- Channel name: `Midnight Tide Sleep`
- Slug: `midnight-tide-sleep`
- Niche: black-screen and soft-motion sleep ambience for late-night wind-down listeners
- Tagline: `Long-form sleep ambience with clean packaging and zero-distraction visuals.`

## First Three Video Concepts

### 1. Launch-01

- Title: `Rain Drift Sleep Music | 60 Minutes Black Screen For Deep Sleep`
- Preset: `rain`
- Release preset: `black-screen`
- Why first: easiest to validate because the packaging is simple and the promise is clear

### 2. Launch-02

- Title: `Ocean Breath Sleep Music | 30 Minutes Soft Ocean Drift Visual`
- Preset: `ocean`
- Release preset: `ocean-drift`
- Why second: strong A/B test against black-screen packaging

### 3. Launch-03

- Title: `Brown Noise Sleep Music | 30 Minutes Rain Window Visual For Focus And Sleep`
- Preset: `brown-noise`
- Release preset: `rain-window`
- Why third: broader search intent across both sleep and focus use cases

## First Publish-Ready Target

The first target to render and upload is `Launch-01`.

Success criteria:

1. render the MP4 bundle from Sleep Lab
2. upload it to YouTube as private or unlisted
3. review thumbnail, mobile playback, and title fit
4. only then publish publicly
