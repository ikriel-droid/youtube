# YouTube Scenic Visual Guide

This guide defines the visual direction for scenic sleep uploads.

## Core Rule

The video should feel calm before it feels descriptive.

That means:

- slower motion
- fewer text blocks
- softer contrast
- cleaner first frame

## Preferred Scenic Modes

### Ocean Drift

- broadest default scenic style
- soft glow and low-frequency movement
- best for the channel house style

### Rain Window

- stronger mood and more specific theme
- good for secondary themed uploads
- works well with brown-noise crossover packaging

### Future Night Sky

- reserved for a later third scenic family
- should only be added if it feels softer than black-screen and less busy than generic star loops

## Thumbnail Direction

- two to four words max
- one mood phrase
- one visual cue
- no dense headline blocks

## First-Frame Direction

- keep title text lighter than the thumbnail
- do not let the video frame look like a slide deck
- scenic uploads should feel like ambience first, packaging second

Decision:

- keep a light scenic headline on the frame
- keep heavier packaging language on the thumbnail / metadata side
- do not let scenic renders become full-screen text cards
