# YouTube Scenic Packaging Guide

This guide locks the first scenic packaging rules so we do not re-decide titles and thumbnails for every upload.

## Lead Phrase

Use `ambient sleep` as the lead creative phrase for scenic uploads.

Why:

- it feels softer than `sleep music`
- it fits scenic ocean and rain-window uploads better
- `sleep music` can still remain in supporting tags and description copy for search coverage

## Ocean Scenic Title Variants

Primary:

- `Ocean Breath Ambient Sleep | 30 Minutes Soft Ocean Drift Visual`

Backup:

- `Ocean Drift Ambient Sleep | 30 Minutes Calm Night Water Visual`
- `Soft Ocean Ambient Sleep | 30 Minutes Gentle Drift For Wind Down`

## Rain-Window Scenic Title Variants

Primary:

- `Rain Window Ambient Sleep | 30 Minutes Brown Noise Night Drift`

Backup:

- `Brown Noise Ambient Sleep | 30 Minutes Rain Window Calm Visual`
- `Rain Drift Ambient Sleep | 30 Minutes Night Window For Sleep`

## Scenic Thumbnail Language

Use short, soft phrases only.

Preferred words:

- `ocean drift`
- `rain window`
- `ambient sleep`
- `night calm`
- `deep rest`

Avoid:

- cluttered keyword stacks
- all-caps urgency
- technical wording like `black screen` unless the video is intentionally utility-first

## Scenic Packaging Rule

For the next public scenic uploads:

- title = one soft lead phrase plus one concrete visual cue
- thumbnail = two to four words max
- description = calm sensory framing first, search keywords second

## Imported Audio Public-Candidate Rule

When the scenic upload uses imported licensed audio:

- prefer a softer broad title over internal source naming
- do not lead with internal workflow words like `licensed audio` or `visual`
- keep the source and license in the description, not the headline

Current preferred imported-ocean packaging:

- title: `Ocean Ambient Sleep | 30 Minutes Coastal Wave Drift`
- thumbnail language: `ocean drift`, `deep rest`, or `coastal calm`
- description lead: calm sensory framing first, source attribution second
