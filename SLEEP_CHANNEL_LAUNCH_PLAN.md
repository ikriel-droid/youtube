# Sleep Channel Launch Plan

## Direction

The channel is no longer just a black-screen or single-format sleep test.

The working direction is now:

- natural healing ambience
- scenic-first visuals
- a repeatable 10-video nature series
- rain included as a core theme, not a side experiment

## Channel Identity

- Channel name: `Midnight Tide Sleep`
- Slug: `midnight-tide-sleep`
- Niche: natural healing ambience built around forests, rain, ocean, waterfalls, wind, and other scenic long-form sleep visuals
- Tagline: `Nature-led sleep ambience with calm motion, real scenery, and gentle long-form healing visuals.`
