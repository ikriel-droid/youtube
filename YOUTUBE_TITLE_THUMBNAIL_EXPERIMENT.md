# YouTube Title And Thumbnail Experiment Plan

The first public upload is our baseline:

- Baseline video ID: `Fb1c63adZZk`
- Baseline title: `Rain Drift Sleep Music | 60 Minutes Black Screen For Deep Sleep`
- Baseline visual style: black screen

## Why This Matters

The first upload validated the release path, but it does not yet tell us whether:

- scenic visuals improve CTR
- a softer title improves browsing clicks
- "black screen" helps search but hurts homepage appeal

## Baseline

- Title: `Rain Drift Sleep Music | 60 Minutes Black Screen For Deep Sleep`
- Thumbnail direction: text-forward sleep utility thumbnail
- Visual direction: black screen

## Alternative A

- Candidate concept: `Launch-02`
- Title: `Ocean Breath Sleep Music | 30 Minutes Soft Ocean Drift Visual`
- Thumbnail direction: calm scenic ocean visual with lighter copy
- Goal: test whether scenic motion improves CTR
- Status: uploaded publicly
- Video ID: `TaOjFxPpfv8`

## Alternative B

- Candidate concept: `Launch-03`
- Title: `Rain Window Ambient Sleep | 30 Minutes Brown Noise Night Drift`
- Thumbnail direction: rain-window scene with broader sleep/focus appeal
- Goal: test whether broader intent beats pure sleep utility packaging
- Status: uploaded publicly
- Video ID: `uXQgjWFzY0I`
- Watch URL: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXQgjWFzY0I`

## What To Compare

- click-through rate
- average view duration
- comments or qualitative feedback
- whether viewers prefer black screen or scenic ambient presentation

## Current Experiment State

- Baseline public upload exists: `Fb1c63adZZk`
- Alternative scenic public uploads exist: `TaOjFxPpfv8`, `uXQgjWFzY0I`
- Black-screen vs scenic direction is considered closed by operator decision
- The remaining open comparison is scenic-vs-scenic, not black-vs-scenic

## Decision Rule

- If `Launch-02` wins CTR clearly, move the channel cover style toward scenic visuals
- If `Launch-03` wins watch duration, lean into rain-window / brown-noise crossover packaging
- If the black-screen baseline wins both, keep it as the utility anchor format and test scenic uploads as supporting formats
